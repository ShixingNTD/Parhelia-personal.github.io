## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a serene, tactile, and deeply editorial digital atmosphere designed for a personal portfolio and professional profile. Rooted in botanical warmth, mindful craft, and the cadence of high-end art monographs, it positions the creator as an intentional, multidisciplinary thinker with a discerning eye.

### Aesthetic Persona
- **Botanical Sanctuary:** Drawing cues from pressed specimens, conservatory architecture, and sun-bleached linen rather than clinical tech interfaces.
- **Editorial Poise:** High-contrast serif headlines paired with italicized poetic asides, balanced by pristine, rhythmic sans-serif body typography.
- **Warm Tactility:** Rice paper underpinnings, quiet clay cards, arched portals, and delicate stone borders that mimic artisanal stationer bindings.

### Emotional Target
The user interface evokes unhurried confidence, quiet luxury, and artistic integrity. Hiring managers, collaborators, and clients should feel as though they are stepping into an airy architect's studio or a curated private greenhouse—grounded, elegant, and entirely memorable.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in unbleached organic materials, deep chlorophyll pigment, and sun-baked mineral accents. 

### Palette Roles
- **Base Canvas (`#F9F8F4` - Warm Alabaster):** A luminous, non-glare surface reminiscent of Japanese kozo paper or handmade cotton rag. It softens screen fatigue and establishes natural warmth.
- **Surface Elevation (`#F2F0EB` & `#DCCFC2` - Soft Clay & Mushroom):** Used for elevated cards, modal panels, and inset modules to provide distinction without harsh brightness shifts.
- **Primary Type & Framing (`#2D3A31` - Deep Forest Green):** Replaces harsh pure black. It grounds typography, icons, and primary interactive states with rich botanical depth.
- **Secondary Tone (`#8C9A84` - Dried Sage):** Serves decorative outlines, secondary navigation, active filter chips, metadata tags, and soft hover fills.
- **Accent Tones (`#C98A75` - Muted Terracotta & `#A89F91` - Weathered Stone):** Used sparingly for focal moments—such as status indicators, current job availability badges, callout highlights, and select italicized lead-ins.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#E6E2DA` - Delicate Stone):** Barely-there tactile boundaries that structure space cleanly without interrupting the flow of text and photography.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy embraces tension between romantic, high-contrast serif letterforms and pragmatic, humanist sans-serif readability.

### Rules of Engagement
- **Editorial Emphasis:** Use `Playfair Display` with italics sparingly within headlines to emphasize key conceptual nouns (e.g., *Crafting*, *Spatial Systems*, *Curated*).
- **Prose Comfort:** Use `Source Sans 3` for all multi-line case studies, project narratives, and UI metadata. Its open counters and organic stroke modulations match the warm, human voice of the design system.
- **Meta & Categorization:** All project roles, dates, and taxonomies must be set in `label-caps` with wide tracking to mirror catalog numbering in gallery folios.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces generous breathing room, non-linear cadence, and asymmetric framing that resembles modern architectural portfolio books.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column asymmetric fluid grid with `5rem` outer page margins and `2.5rem` gutters. Content blocks alternate between centered narrow narrative columns (spanning 8 columns) and dual-span case study showcases (spanning 5 and 7 columns).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `2.5rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Image-text pairs collapse smoothly into staggered vertical blocks.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` outer canvas padding. Portals, project titles, and imagery span full width to ensure generous touch zones and uncompressed visual impact.

### Rhythmic Padding
Section padding relies on `space-2xl` for vertical breathing room between major case studies, ensuring each project feels like an individual exhibition room rather than a continuous feed.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy eschews synthetic drop shadows in favor of tonal layering, soft paper stacking, and subtle ambient diffusion.

### Principles of Depth
- **Tonal Stepping:** Surfaces layer naturally. The Warm Alabaster foundation (`#F9F8F4`) supports the Soft Clay surface (`#F2F0EB`), which in turn holds floating elements in Mushroom (`#DCCFC2`).
- **Sunlit Ambient Diffusion:** When an element must float (such as floating bottom navbars or interactive project lightboxes), use a multi-stage ambient blur tinted in warm taupe-green rather than charcoal:
  `box-shadow: 0 12px 36px -8px rgba(45, 58, 49, 0.07), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(45, 58, 49, 0.03);`
- **Delicate Stone Outlines:** Floating panels utilize a 1px solid border in `#E6E2DA` to softly define boundaries against varying screen calibrations without creating harsh delineations.
- **Tactile Paper Grain:** Background layers may feature a CSS or SVG paper-fiber micro-noise (1-2% opacity) to eliminate digital sterility and simulate fine stationery.

## Shapes

The shape system completely eliminates sharp 90-degree corners, favoring biological, fluid, and architectural curves.

### Curvature Taxonomy
- **The Arched Portal Silhouette:** Hero imagery, featured case study previews, and profile portraits should employ custom arched top geometries (`border-radius: 9999px 9999px 2rem 2rem` or `rounded-t-full rounded-b-3xl`) to recall classical conservatory windows and plant press mounts.
- **Organic Containers:** Standard cards, bio panels, and content blocks use sweeping `2rem` to `2.5rem` (32px - 40px) corner radii.
- **Capsule Contours (Pill-Shaped):** All buttons, status chips, tag labels, and input bars adopt a full pill radius (`rounded-full` / 9999px).

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Links
- **Primary Action (Pill Button):** Solid `#2D3A31` (Deep Forest Green) background with `#F9F8F4` (Warm Alabaster) text, set in `label-md`. Padding: `0.875rem 2rem`. Subtle scale transformation (`scale-[1.02]`) and background shift to `#3D4C42` on hover.
- **Secondary Action (Outlined Capsule):** Transparent background with a 1px solid `#8C9A84` border and `#2D3A31` text. On hover, fills softly with `#F2F0EB`.
- **Text Link:** Set in `body-md` with an italicized serif flourish or a thin `#8C9A84` underline offset by `4px`, transitioning to `#2D3A31` upon focus.

### Project & Case Study Cards
- **Structure:** Encased in `#F2F0EB` with `border: 1px solid #E6E2DA` and a 32px corner radius.
- **Media Frame:** Images nestled within top-arched silhouettes (`rounded-t-[32px] rounded-b-2xl`) with an unhurried zoom transition (`duration-700 ease-out`) on card hover.
- **Content Block:** Generous internal padding (`2rem`), featuring `label-caps` year/role, `headline-md` title, and a brief description in `body-md`.

### Status Badges & Chips
- **Availability Pill:** Soft Sage background (`rgba(140, 154, 132, 0.15)`) with a pulsating 6px solid dot in `#8C9A84` alongside text reading *"Available for Select Projects"* in `label-caps`.
- **Category Filter Chips:** Fully rounded pills in `#F2F0EB`, bordered by `#E6E2DA`. Active state transitions to `#2D3A31` with `#F9F8F4` text.

### Form Inputs & Contact Modals
- **Input Fields:** Pill-shaped text fields or generously curved (`1.25rem`) multi-line areas with `#F9F8F4` fills and subtle `#E6E2DA` borders.
- **Focus State:** 1px ring in `#8C9A84` accompanied by a gentle Warm Forest glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 4px rgba(140, 154, 132, 0.12)`).
- **Placeholders:** Styled in muted earth grey (`#9C978E`), switching to `#2D3A31` upon input entry.

### Architectural Testimonial Quote
- Centered, oversized container in `#DCCFC2` / soft mushroom with `rounded-[40px]`.
- Houses large pull-quotes in `editorial-quote` (`Playfair Display Italic`), framed by a faint terracotta (`#C98A75`) opening glyph.
